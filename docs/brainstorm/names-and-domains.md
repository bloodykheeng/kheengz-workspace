# Names and domains

Checked 15 Nov 2026 through RDAP (the registries' public lookup). "Free" means not registered; confirm price at a
registrar (Cloudflare, Namecheap) since some names are premium. `.ug` cannot be checked this way: ask the `.ug`
registry or a local registrar.

## Two names, not one

- **The platform** (what you sell to shop owners): your brand.
- **Each shop** (what buyers see): the client's brand. Fahim's is **Home Nest**. Buyers never need to spell the
  platform's name, so it can be personal.

## My view

**Kheeng**, with **`kheeng.com`**, and the two editions named from it:

- **Kheeng Shop**: the single vendor edition.
- **Kheeng Market**: the marketplace edition.

It keeps your identity, is spelled the way it is said once heard, and the `.com` is free. If `kheengz.com` is
already yours, **Kheengz** is equally good (same editions: Kheengz Shop / Kheengz Market) and you keep the plural
you wanted. Kheengdom is the fun one for a tagline ("Your shop. Your kheengdom.") rather than the product name.
